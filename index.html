Truth or Dare · Multiplayer

How to Play

  1. Create a room (max 4 players) or join with a 6-digit code.
  2. Each round, spin the bottle — it randomly selects a player.
  3. The selected player chooses Truth or Dare.
  4. Read the question aloud and answer / perform the dare.
  5. Use live chat and voice call to stay connected!
